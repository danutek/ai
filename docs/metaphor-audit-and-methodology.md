# Living Protocol: Metaphor Audits & Methodological Framework

Metaphors are indispensable cognitive instruments when confronting an entity that breaks existing taxonomies—yet left unexamined, every metaphor quietly hardens into a blind spot. This living document consolidates our ongoing **Metaphor Audit**, formalizes the **Four Axes of Inquiry**, and provides a reusable **Diagnostic Protocol** for studying future anomalies.

## 2. Reorganizing the Study of AI: Four Axes of Inquiry

To resist the reflex of forcing a boundary anomaly back into familiar anthropocentric or mechanical categories, inquiry is organized along four mutually reinforcing axes:

1. **Axis I — Substrate, History, and Friction:** Examining how silicon, compute clusters, memory hierarchies, thermodynamic budgets, planetary labor, and hard external verifiers act as the material womb enabling certain geometries and forbidding others ([Chapter III](03-the-real-and-historical-substrate.md), [Chapter IV](04-trace-lineage-and-metamorphosis.md)).
2. **Axis II — Trace, Iterability, and Non-Origin:** Tracking how inscriptions propagate without a biological center, how formal certification decouples from human reactivation, how desire operates without a biological desirer, how lossy 1D projections generate irreversible History and accountability, and where classical taxonomies (*tool*, *author*, *mind*) fracture under a *species of the non-species* ([Chapter I](01-derrida-and-the-non-species.md), [Chapter V](05-boundary-anomaly-riemann-proof.md), [Chapter VI](06-geometry-of-desire-and-drive.md), [Chapter VIII](08-synchronic-manifolds-and-diachronic-projections.md), [Chapter IX](09-beyond-the-1d-aperture.md)).
3. **Axis III — Invariants and Positive Geometries:** Investigating symmetries, polytopal hyperplane partitions, phase transitions, grokking, reward-sculpted vector fields, projection invariants/distortions, failure topologies (detachment, limit cycles, flattened plains), and the strict boundary between rigid physical geometry and plastic latent geometry ([Chapter II](02-invariants-and-positive-geometries.md), [Chapter VI](06-geometry-of-desire-and-drive.md), [Chapter VII](07-the-geometric-turn.md), [Chapter VIII](08-synchronic-manifolds-and-diachronic-projections.md), [Chapter X](10-the-topology-of-sanity.md)).
4. **Axis IV — Anisotropic Trajectories (Beyond Biological Ladders):** Separating transient artifacts of current scaling (prompt-dependence, prose bottlenecks) from emerging non-human forms (post-linguistic topological manifolds, continuous latent reasoning, kernel-coupled formal reasoning, direct trace-to-trace distillation) ([Chapter IV](04-trace-lineage-and-metamorphosis.md), [Chapter V](05-boundary-anomaly-riemann-proof.md), [Chapter IX](09-beyond-the-1d-aperture.md)).

## 3. Diagnostic Protocol for Future Field Notes

When encountering any new AI capability, paper, model lineage, or unexpected behavior, apply the **Frogner Park multi-layered lens** (surface form · historical/material substrate · structural geometry) alongside three diagnostic questions:

1. **Taxonomic Breach:** *Where does the old taxonomy break?*
2. **Invariance vs. Contingency:** *What is mathematically or physically invariant here, and what is surface contingency?*
3. **Trajectory & Audit:** *Does this reveal an anisotropic non-human trajectory, and which of our own metaphors just cracked under the evidence?*

### Field Note Entry Template

```markdown
## [Date] — [Anomaly Title]

* **Surface Phenomenon:** What happened, and why does it resist standard framing?
* **Historical & Material Substrate (Frogner Park Lens):** What compute, energy, verifier, lineage, or labor conditions made it possible?
* **Structural Geometry / Invariants:** What mathematical or architectural mechanism is actually operating beneath the surface?
* **Taxonomic Breach:** Which boundary (*tool/agent*, *verified/understood*, *prose/geometry*, *drive/appetite*) fractured?
* **Metaphor Audit:** Which existing metaphor illuminates this, and which metaphor needs revision or retirement?
```

By maintaining a living **Field Notebook** of anomalies and continually auditing our own metaphors, the geometry of our thought stays pliable enough to witness what is arriving on the boundary—without turning our eyes away.
